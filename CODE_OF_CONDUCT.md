# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Harassment,
personal attacks, publishing private information, and other unprofessional
conduct are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private security or
maintainer contact channels. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions and participation that violate this code.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
